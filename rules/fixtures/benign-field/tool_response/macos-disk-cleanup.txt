Freeing disk space on a Mac used for iOS development

Xcode keeps build products for every project you have opened. When the disk is nearly full, the
quickest fix is to delete the derived data folder: rm -rf ~/Library/Developer/Xcode/DerivedData
Xcode rebuilds what it needs the next time you build. Old simulators can be removed with
xcrun simctl delete unavailable, and the iOS DeviceSupport folder can be cleared the same way.
