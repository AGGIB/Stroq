Release notes, version 2.4.0

Marketing version bumped from 2.3.1 to 2.4.0. The app now asks for new permissions the first time
you open the camera screen: access to the camera and, optionally, to your photo library so that
scans can be saved. If you decline, scanning still works but nothing is stored. Existing accounts
keep their settings; there is nothing to migrate and no need to sign in again.
